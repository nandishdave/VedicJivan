Chart-Strength Methodology

How the 8 factors are calculated & ranked, and how the composite score is built — VedicJivan famous-vs-ordinary study.

Read this first — the honest boundary. This system does not predict fame. Across 128 real charts (32 famous + 96 ordinary) the best out-of-sample score is CV-AUC ≈ 0.55 — a weak tilt, only a little above a coin flip (0.50), well short of "useful" (0.65+). Treat every number here as a chart-strength readout, never a verdict on a person.

The pipeline in one picture

each chart ──▶ 8 factors ──▶ orient each "famous-positive" ──▶ standardise (z-score)

The headline score is simply how many of the 8 factors a chart stacks above par. That co-occurrence count separated famous from ordinary better than any weighted model we tried.

1Dasha timing (ages 20–50)

Vedic astrology holds that when a strong period runs matters more than the static snapshot. For each Vimshottari mahādashā overlapping ages 20–50:

strength = min(dasha_lord_shadbala_ratio / 1.5, 1.0) × 100
if lord is a natural benefic (Jupiter/Venus/Mercury) or the Lagna lord:
        strength = min(strength × 1.15, 100)      # a light bonus
factor = Σ(strength × overlap_years) / Σ(overlap_years)   # time-weighted average

Higher = strong, benefic-flavoured periods ran through the productive years.

2Dhana yoga  ·  3Prosperity yoga

Both use the shared graded engine (below). Dhana = associations among the core wealth lords 1 / 2 / 11 (Lagnesh / Dhanesh / Labhesh). Prosperity = any association that also touches the 5th or 9th lord (purva-punya / fortune) — scored separately, never mixed into Dhana.

link score = connection-type × lord-multiplier × dignity × house-of-formation
factor      = Σ (all qualifying links)

4Raja yoga

Association of a Kendra lord (1/4/7/10) with a Trikona lord (1/5/9). The 6/8/12 lords are not partners. Same engine, plus a combustion penalty:

link score = connection × lord-grade × dignity × house × combustion
lord-grade : 9th+10th (Dharma-Karmadhipati) 1.30 · any pair with the 1st 1.15 · else 1.0
combustion : × 0.5 for each yoga-planet within the Sun's combustion orb
             (dignity alone can't catch a "burnt" planet)

Example — a chart with a 9th↔10th parivartana (a "supreme" raja yoga on paper) but the 10th lord debilitated and the 9th lord combust scores only ≈2.1, not the ≈13 the raw combination suggests.

6 & 7 Ashtakavarga (1st & 10th houses)

Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) sums each sign's benefic bindus (0–56, average ~28). Factor 6 = bindus on the 1st house (self/vitality); factor 7 = bindus on the 10th (career/status). More bindus = more supported house.

From factors to the composite score

The weighted linear model (logistic regression over the 8 factors) actually underperformed — it fought itself with negative weights. A simpler idea won:

1. ORIENT   — flip each factor so "higher = more famous-like" (using its lift sign)
2. STANDARDISE — z-score each factor across all 128 charts
3. COUNT    — how many of the 8 are elevated (z > 0)   →   the score, shown "X / 8"
4. TIE-BREAK — sum of the 8 z-scores (a continuous composite)
Why a count, not a weighted sum? The signal is co-occurrence: famous charts tend to stack more strong factors at once, rather than having one factor amplify another. Adding multiplicative interaction terms only overfit the data (it made things worse), confirming there's no synergy to exploit — just "how many line up."

Bottom line. These 8 graded factors are faithful chart-strength tools — genuinely useful for individual chart analysis and for ranking auspicious birth-moments (Muhurta) by the quality of the yogas forming. They are not fame predictors, and no honest reading of the data makes them one.
