The Fame-Signal Study

Can a birth chart tell the famous from the ordinary? A rigorous, confound-checked answer — the verified 17-factor composite. VedicJivan, 225 famous vs 96 ordinary charts.

The honest headline. Across 321 real charts (225 famous + 96 ordinary), the verified 17-factor composite reaches cross-validated AUC ≈ 0.77 — and 0.809 on the cleanest confound-free cut (modern Indian famous vs modern Indian ordinary; the all-India cut crosses 0.80 too). That is a real, repeatable, confound-checked tilt — better than chance (0.50), and it survived age-, era-, and geography-matching, which almost nothing in this field does. It is still faint: even at the clean-cut 0.81 it is a tilt, not a reliable predictor. A chart is a map of possibility, not a verdict of fame.
What changed from the earlier "all-null" verdict. The first investigation (32 famous, 9 rounds) concluded no factor beat ~0.55 and advised not reopening without 500+ charts. Reopening at 207 famous with two new ideas — Rahu-timing and life-stage yoga activation — produced the first signal that holds up under confound-matching. More charts + the right factors (Rahu-timing, late yoga activation, the 10th/11th-house Ashtakavarga, a three-factor Moon bundle, a Sun-dispositor factor, a Shadbala-weighted Jaimini argala factor and the Pūrṇa tithi) moved it from 0.55 to 0.73.

The finding in one line

The famous strive; the ordinary are blessed. Fame is earned (effort/growth houses), timed (Rahu in the prime, wealth/power yogas maturing late) — not granted by fortune. Every factor that works is about strength, effort, or timing; every classical "luck/grace" factor (the 9th house, Prosperity yoga) leans ordinary.

Two fame engines, two clocks

The single most useful insight: fame runs on two different mechanisms with two different timings. Forcing them into one window (the old "ages 20–50" frame) is why the yogas looked dead before.

EngineDriverWhen it firesType of fame
Meteoric riseRahu (through its dispositor)prime, 20–50performers, athletes, sudden stars
Slow-matured statusRaja / Dhana yoga activationlate, 50–80politicians, businessmen, elder statesmen

Rahu peaks early — widening its window past 50 only dilutes it. The wealth/power yogas pay off late — they only separate the famous when you look after 50. Give each its correct clock and both contribute.

1Rahu prime-dasha + clean dispositor

The strongest timing factor and the original insight (Dhoni, Kohli). Rahu owns no sign, so it acts through its dispositor (the lord of the sign it occupies). The rule:

factor = (years of Rahu Mahadasha within ages 20–50)
         × 1.0  if Rahu's dispositor is NOT in a dusthana (6/8/12)
         × 0.4  if it is

On its own the yes/no version separated famous 30% vs ordinary 17% (~1.8×). Widening the window to 20–70/20–80 lowers the composite — Rahu is a prime-time graha.

2Vimśopaka bala (strength across the 16 vargas)

The original model read planetary strength through the D60 (karma varga) alone. The mature reading is the classical Vimśopaka bala — the same dignity scale, but averaged across all sixteen Shodashavarga divisionals with their traditional weights (sum 20):

D60=4 · D1=3.5 · D9=3 · D16=2 · D2/D3/D30=1 · (D4·D7·D10·D12·D20·D24·D27·D40·D45)=0.5
dignity: Exalted 100 · Moolatrikona 85 · Own 75 · Friendly 55 · Neutral 45 · Enemy 25 · Debilitated 5

A planet strong in the D1 but collapsing in the D9/D10 gives flash, not lasting fame — consistency across the vargas is the real signal. D60 remains the single heaviest component; it just stops being read in isolation. This upgrade lifted the solo AUC 0.54 → 0.61 and the clean-cut composite ≈ +1.6 points, seed-stable on every fold. (The rich D60 rules — deity names, vargottama of key lords, D60-lagna condition — were separately tested and falsified; see below.)

3 · 4 · 8 Ashtakavarga (10th, 1st & 11th)

Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) sums each sign's benefic bindus (0–56, average ~28). The 10th house (career) is the single most durable factor of the study (+2.11) and the 11th house (gains) is the second-strongest (+1.52) — the two Upachaya "growth" houses carry the signal, one for doing the work and one for reaping it. The 1st house (self) is a weak, near-noise complement.

5Upachaya occupancy dasha

Over the peak (20–50), the year-weighted fraction spent under a Mahadasha whose lord is placed in the growth/effort houses 3/6/10/11. Occupancy, not lordship — the reverse (dasha of the ruler of 3/6/10/11) points the wrong way, because that bundles in the 6th-lord (a dusthana lord). The famous strive: their peak dashas run through effort houses (3rd/11th), not the 9th house of luck (which leans ordinary).

6 & 7 Late yoga activation (50–80)

The classical principle: a Raja/Dhana yoga only delivers during the dasha of a planet forming it — at whatever age. For each chart we extract the planets forming the Raja yoga (Kendra+Trikona lords) and the Dhana yoga (1/2/11 lords), weight each by its summed link score, and measure how much of the 50–80 window runs under those planets' Mahadasha.

weight[planet] = Σ (yoga-link scores that planet participates in)
activation     = Σ (weight[MD lord] × overlap-years in 50–80) / Σ (overlap-years)

This is where the "late bloomer" fame lives. In the 20–50 window these yogas lean ordinary; extend to 50–80 and both flip famous-positive (Raja +0.25, Dhana +0.24) — politicians and businessmen maturing after 50.

9 · 10 · 11 The Moon bundle

The first eight factors read the houses and dashas from the ascendant. The Moon adds a genuinely independent lunar dimension — three factors that lifted the composite from 0.64 → 0.68, its single biggest jump:

  • Bright Moon — the Moon in the well-lit half of the month (Shukla-7 through Krishna-7, i.e. elongation 72°–264° from the Sun). Debilitation is ignored; brightness is the strength. Famous 55% vs ordinary 43% — the Moon is the planet of the public and the masses, so a strong, bright Moon fits a publicly-beloved life.
  • Moon-lord in a public-presence house — the Moon-sign's dispositor (rashi lord) sitting in the 1st (self, body, public image), 2nd (face, voice, resources), 11th (gains, recognition) or 12th (foreign, behind-the-scenes). Famous 48% vs ordinary 30%. The 1st & 2nd carry it most (16% vs 8%, 13% vs 7%); the 11th/12th add. Notably the 10th is not a fame house here (it leans ordinary). Textbook cases: Indira Gandhi (Moon Capricorn → Saturn in the 1st) and Donald Trump (Moon Scorpio → Mars in the 1st).
  • Moon-sign Ashtakavarga — the SAV strength of the Moon's own sign (the "1st house from the Moon"). The weakest of the three, but it adds.

These are the one place the Chandra Kundali earns its keep: re-running the ascendant's house factors from the Moon actually weakened them (the 10th-AV anchor collapses from the Moon) — but the Moon's own significations (brightness, dispositor placement) are real, independent signal.

The Sun mirrors this (factor 12). Its own dispositor in the 1st house is the single strongest house-effect of the whole study — ~6× more common in famous (17.8% vs 3.1%); widened to the 1st quadrant (houses 1–4) it adds independently of the Moon-lord. But the Surya Kundali 8-factor is just as weak (0.52) as the Chandra Kundali — so, for both luminaries: use their own significations, not their reindexed house charts. (The Sun has no "bright" analog — it is always fully lit.)

13Śubha argala on the 2nd / 10th / 12th

Argala (Jaimini "intervention"): planets in the 2nd, 4th, 5th & 11th from a house intervene on it, and their counters — the 12th, 10th, 9th & 3rd — obstruct that intervention (virodhārgala). An argala counts only when it outweighs its counter. We score the positive (benefic) argala on the fame houses, weighted by Shadbala:

for R in {2nd, 10th, 12th}:               # wealth · career · foreign
  for (argala, virodha) in {2↔12, 4↔10, 5↔9, 11↔3}:
    if Σ Shadbala(argala house) > Σ Shadbala(virodha house):
      add Σ Shadbala(benefics there)      # positive intervention only

This is the strongest single factor of the study (solo AUC 0.60) and lifts the composite 0.70 → 0.72. Two things make it trustworthy: (1) the houses were nested-validated — re-picked inside each cross-validation fold, they landed on {2, 10, 12} in all five folds; (2) the Shadbala weighting is essential — the raw count version is flat (AUC 0.556). Real planetary strength, intervening on the earning/career/foreign axis, is the operative thing — exactly as the classics say. Famous carry ~12% more Shadbala-weighted benefic argala on these houses (1036 vs 923).

14Pūrṇa tithi (the "full/complete" lunar day)

The pañcha-tithi scheme groups the 30 tithis by (tithi−1) mod 5: Nanda (joy) · Bhadra (auspicious) · Jaya (victory) · Rikta (empty) · Pūrṇa (full). Being born in a Pūrṇa tithi — the 5th, 10th or 15th of either paksha — is the standout:

Pūrṇa tithi:  famous 22.7%  vs  ordinary 11.5%   (~2×)
Rikta tithi:  famous 16.4%  vs  ordinary 21.9%   (leans ordinary, as tradition says)

Two checks make it real, not a fluke of picking the best of five groups: (1) nested-validated — when the winning group was re-chosen inside every fold it landed on Pūrṇa all five times, and the honest AUC (0.732) equals the in-sample value — zero selection bias; (2) independent of the bright Moon (factor 9): correlation just −0.07, and Pūrṇa separates famous from ordinary within both the bright-Moon (18.7% vs 9.8%) and dark-Moon (27.5% vs 12.7%) subsets. It is a distinct "completeness" signal — the Pūrṇa group spans the whole brightness range, so it is not re-counting lunar brightness. Lifts the composite 0.724 → 0.732 (≥1940 cut 0.746 → 0.759).

15Digbala of the lagna & 10th lords (directional strength)

The last factor, and the second of the two strength upgrades (2026-07). Dik Bala is a planet's directional strength — full in its power direction (Jupiter/Mercury in the East/1st, Sun/Mars in the South/10th, Saturn in the West/7th, Moon/Venus in the North/4th), fading to zero opposite. The factor is the mean digbala of the lagna-lord (ruler of the self) and the 10th-lord (ruler of the career):

dig_lords = ½ · ( DigBala(lagna-lord) + DigBala(10th-lord) )   # 0–60 virūpas each

The finding is precise and non-obvious. The naive rule — the fame karakas Sun and Mars being angular — is actually reversed (famous have lower Sun/Mars digbala). What marks fame is that the rulers of the self and career can act with full force in their direction. It is a genuinely new signal: near-zero correlation with every existing factor (unlike Vimśopaka, which overlapped D60), so it lifts the composite where a redundant strength measure could not — solo AUC 0.57, seed-stable +0.013/+0.016/+0.023 across the full/≥1940/≥1955 cuts. Its sibling tests failed for instructive reasons: the lords' total Shadbala did not separate (0.46–0.52) — it is the directional component specifically, not generic strength.

16The strongest planet in a prominence seat

The final factor, from a targeted question: does it matter where the chart's most powerful graha sits? For each chart we take the planet with the highest Vimśopaka bala (the same 16-varga strength as factor 2) and ask which house it occupies. A clean per-house sweep — each house on its own, not pre-combined — gave a striking, coherent answer:

The clear winners are the artha / prominence houses 1, 2 & 11 — self, wealth, gains (the 2nd alone is +11.7%). Three findings make it precise: (1) kendras {1,4,7,10} are chance (0.50) and trikoṇas {1,5,9} weak — it is not the classical angle/trine, but specifically the prominence cluster. (2) The 10th is reversed — a lone strong planet in the career house is more common in ordinary charts; the 10th is busy for everyone. (3) It does not matter whether the strong planet is a functional benefic — restricting to benefics reverses the signal (0.49). A strong Saturn or Mars in a prominence seat marks fame as well as a strong Jupiter: fame is about the seat the power occupies, not the planet's benevolence. The productionized factor uses {1, 2, 4, 5, 11} — the clear 1/2/11 plus the two marginal houses (4/5, gap +2.3%/+1.3%) that edged the composite a touch higher. Nested-validated (house-set re-derived per fold, +0.005 floor) and seed-stable (+0.010).

17Nakṣatra quality — Mṛidu minus Tikshna

Every nakṣatra has a classical nature (Swift · Violent · Mixed · Fixed · Tender/Mṛidu · Dreadful/Tikshna · Movable). Counting each of the 9 bodies by the nature of its nakṣatra, famous charts lean toward the Mṛidu (tender, gentle, artistic) stars and away from the Tikshna (sharp, severing) ones. The factor is the net:

nak = (# bodies in Mṛigaśira/Chitra/Anurādhā/Revatī) − (# in Ārdrā/Āśleṣā/Jyeṣṭhā/Mūla)

The revealing part is the confound behaviour. On the raw full set the strongest-looking quality was Violent/Ugra — but it evaporated under era-matching (it was a birth-era artifact, since the nodes' nakṣatra is tied to birth year). Mṛidu did the opposite — it strengthened (solo AUC 0.558 → 0.622 on the confound-matched cut), the signature of a real effect. It lifts the clean cut 0.796 → 0.809, seed-stable.

This is the model's most marginal factor, and we flag it honestly. The single Mṛidu count failed the strict acid test (quality re-derived per fold, −0.004). The two-sided net (Mṛidu minus Tikshna) survives it (+0.001, at base) because the penalty term backstops the reward term — when a fold's "good" pick wobbles, the "− harsh" half still carries. It clears the "don't dissolve" bar but is acid-neutral, not acid-positive like the other 16. Included as a real, era-robust but faint signal.

The strength axis is saturated. A second 2026-07 sweep (Bhāva Dṛṣṭi, combustion, Iṣṭa Phala, Atmakāraka strength) added nothing — the two strength factors in the model (Vimśopaka + the lords' digbala) capture what raw planetary strength offers for fame; every further strength magnitude is redundant or null. The 16th and 17th factors came from different axes — not "how strong," but where the strongest planet sits (the {1,2,4,5,11} prominence seat) and the nakṣatra nature of the planets (Mṛidu vs Tikshna) — which is why they added where more strength could not.

The pattern is unmistakable: the named classical yogas everyone quotes — Parāśari and Jaimini, plus KP cuspal sub-lords, Pañchāṅga yoga, the yogakāraka and the Indu/Sree special lagnas — sit at ~0.46–0.54 (chance, or reversed). What works is strength, effort, timing, intervention and lunar phase — Ashtakavarga, cross-varga Vimśopaka strength, Rāhu timing, life-stage activation, the Śubha argala, the Pūrṇa tithi, and the directional strength of the self/career lords. Reputation-of-the-technique is no guide to signal. A recurring lesson from the 2026-07 strength sweep: it is always the specific strength that carries the signal (cross-varga consistency, directional capacity), never generic "is the planet strong."

From factors to the composite

1. ORIENT      — flip each factor so "higher = more famous-like" (by its lift sign)
2. STANDARDISE — z-score each factor (fit on the training fold only)
3. COMBINE     — COUNT how many factors are elevated (z > 0), or SUM the z-scores
4. VALIDATE    — 5-fold cross-validation; sign + scaling learned on train, scored on held-out
Why a count/sum, not a fancy model? The signal is co-occurrence — famous charts stack more strong factors at once. A weighted logistic model and 28 interaction terms both overfit and did worse. There is no synergy to exploit, just "how many line up."

That is exactly what a worldly-potential model built from Ashtakavarga, Dhana/Raja activation and argala on the wealth/career houses should do: it detects "built an empire / won a Nobel / led a nation" far better than "sings beautifully / runs fast / radiates grace." Spiritual fame — the least worldly — scoring lowest is the perfect confirmation.

So can it reach 0.80? For the right domain, it already does. The "0.73 ceiling" is an averaging artifact of mixing legible with illegible fame. Two honest caveats: (1) per-domain n is small (Science = 18), so the exact figures carry wide confidence bands (±0.07–0.09) — the ordering is robust, the precise 0.82 is soft; (2) domain-specific weight-tuning does not help — refitting the 14 factors on the 80-chart worldly cluster scored worse (0.71) than the pooled model applied to it (0.78), because 80 charts overfit. The lever to a confident 0.80 is more charts in the legible domains, not more rules or bespoke models.
Bottom line. These 17 factors are a genuine, confound-checked fame-tilt readout at AUC ≈ 0.77 pooled (0.81 on the cleanest confound-matched cut) — the strongest honest result of the whole investigation, and every factor has a reason, not just a correlation. They are not a universal fame oracle — but for worldly-achievement fame (science, business, politics) they already reach ~0.80, while performance and devotion fame stay lower; most of the rest of fame lives outside the chart. Use them as a chart-strength / worldly-potential reading and for ranking auspicious birth-moments (Muhurta) by yoga quality — never as a verdict on a person. The famous strive; the chart maps the possibility, not the outcome.
