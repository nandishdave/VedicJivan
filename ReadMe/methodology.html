The Fame-Signal Study

Can a birth chart tell the famous from the ordinary? A rigorous, confound-checked answer — the verified 7-factor composite. VedicJivan, 207 famous vs 96 ordinary charts.

The honest headline. Across 303 real charts (207 famous + 96 ordinary), the verified 7-factor composite reaches cross-validated AUC ≈ 0.63 — and 0.649 on the cleanest confound-free cut (modern Indian famous vs modern Indian ordinary). That is a real, repeatable, confound-checked tilt — better than chance (0.50), and it survived age-, era-, and geography-matching, which almost nothing in this field does. It is still faint: nowhere near a reliable predictor (0.80+). A chart is a map of possibility, not a verdict of fame.
What changed from the earlier "all-null" verdict. The first investigation (32 famous, 9 rounds) concluded no factor beat ~0.55 and advised not reopening without 500+ charts. Reopening at 207 famous with two new ideas — Rahu-timing and life-stage yoga activation — produced the first signal that holds up under confound-matching. More charts + the right factors moved it from 0.55 to 0.63.

The finding in one line

The famous strive; the ordinary are blessed. Fame is earned (effort/growth houses), timed (Rahu in the prime, wealth/power yogas maturing late) — not granted by fortune. Every factor that works is about strength, effort, or timing; every classical "luck/grace" factor (the 9th house, Prosperity yoga) leans ordinary.

Two fame engines, two clocks

The single most useful insight: fame runs on two different mechanisms with two different timings. Forcing them into one window (the old "ages 20–50" frame) is why the yogas looked dead before.

EngineDriverWhen it firesType of fame
Meteoric riseRahu (through its dispositor)prime, 20–50performers, athletes, sudden stars
Slow-matured statusRaja / Dhana yoga activationlate, 50–80politicians, businessmen, elder statesmen

Rahu peaks early — widening its window past 50 only dilutes it. The wealth/power yogas pay off late — they only separate the famous when you look after 50. Give each its correct clock and both contribute.

1Rahu prime-dasha + clean dispositor

The strongest timing factor and the original insight (Dhoni, Kohli). Rahu owns no sign, so it acts through its dispositor (the lord of the sign it occupies). The rule:

factor = (years of Rahu Mahadasha within ages 20–50)
         × 1.0  if Rahu's dispositor is NOT in a dusthana (6/8/12)
         × 0.4  if it is

On its own the yes/no version separated famous 30% vs ordinary 17% (~1.8×). Widening the window to 20–70/20–80 lowers the composite — Rahu is a prime-time graha.

2D60 (Shashtiamsa) dignity

The finest divisional chart — the "karma" varga. Factor = average dignity of the 7 classical planets in the D60:

Exalted 100 · Moolatrikona 85 · Own 75 · Friendly 55 · Neutral 45 · Enemy 25 · Debilitated 5

The crude average is the version that works. The elaborate classical D60 rules — shashtiamsa deity names, vargottama of key lords, D60-lagna condition, yoga-repeats — were all tested and falsified (see below): they add noise, not signal.

3 & 4 Ashtakavarga (10th & 1st)

Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) sums each sign's benefic bindus (0–56, average ~28). The 10th house (career) is the single most durable factor of the entire study (+2.11); the 1st house (self) is a weak complement.

5Upachaya occupancy dasha

Over the peak (20–50), the year-weighted fraction spent under a Mahadasha whose lord is placed in the growth/effort houses 3/6/10/11. Occupancy, not lordship — the reverse (dasha of the ruler of 3/6/10/11) points the wrong way, because that bundles in the 6th-lord (a dusthana lord). The famous strive: their peak dashas run through effort houses (3rd/11th), not the 9th house of luck (which leans ordinary).

6 & 7 Late yoga activation (50–80)

The classical principle: a Raja/Dhana yoga only delivers during the dasha of a planet forming it — at whatever age. For each chart we extract the planets forming the Raja yoga (Kendra+Trikona lords) and the Dhana yoga (1/2/11 lords), weight each by its summed link score, and measure how much of the 50–80 window runs under those planets' Mahadasha.

weight[planet] = Σ (yoga-link scores that planet participates in)
activation     = Σ (weight[MD lord] × overlap-years in 50–80) / Σ (overlap-years)

This is where the "late bloomer" fame lives. In the 20–50 window these yogas lean ordinary; extend to 50–80 and both flip famous-positive (Raja +0.25, Dhana +0.24) — politicians and businessmen maturing after 50.

What got falsified (equally important)

The pattern is unmistakable: the named classical yogas everyone quotes sit at ~0.48–0.53 (chance). What works is strength, effort, and timing — Ashtakavarga, D60 dignity, Rahu timing, life-stage activation.

From factors to the composite

1. ORIENT      — flip each factor so "higher = more famous-like" (by its lift sign)
2. STANDARDISE — z-score each factor (fit on the training fold only)
3. COMBINE     — COUNT how many factors are elevated (z > 0), or SUM the z-scores
4. VALIDATE    — 5-fold cross-validation; sign + scaling learned on train, scored on held-out
Why a count/sum, not a fancy model? The signal is co-occurrence — famous charts stack more strong factors at once. A weighted logistic model and 28 interaction terms both overfit and did worse. There is no synergy to exploit, just "how many line up."

Confound-matched cuts (the trustworthy numbers)

The full set carries an age gap (famous born ~1940, ordinary ~1987). Stripping it out by matching on era and geography is the real test:

The signal held — and even ticked up — once age and geography were removed. That is the strongest evidence it is real astrology, not a demographic artifact. The wobble at the tightest cut (0.570) keeps us honest: this lives in a ~0.57–0.65 band, not a hard point.

Bottom line. These 7 factors are a genuine, confound-checked fame-tilt readout at AUC ≈ 0.63 — the strongest honest result of the whole investigation, and every factor has a reason, not just a correlation. They are not a fame oracle (0.80+ is out of reach — most of fame lives outside the chart). Use them as a chart-strength / worldly-potential reading and for ranking auspicious birth-moments (Muhurta) by yoga quality — never as a verdict on a person. The famous strive; the chart maps the possibility, not the outcome.
